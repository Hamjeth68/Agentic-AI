# Contract Review Policy

Auto-storage is allowed only when the extracted contract confidence is at least 0.85, all required fields are present, required clauses are present, and no high-severity risks are identified. Required fields are company name, counterparty name, effective date, expiry date, governing law, termination notice, and liability cap.

Contracts with unknown document type, missing fields, missing signatures, ambiguous dates, or parsing uncertainty must be routed to human review.
